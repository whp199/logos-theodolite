=== pass_criterion
Fixed 29 October 2026, before the trial, and encoded in trial_read.py GATES. Every figure is read at 48 pieces with every answer counted. At L45 the neurons are the 1,022 fresh ones and the controls are all 1,024 of the run's.

L45: the design passes only if G1-G5 all hold.
- G1, lead: mass_c AUC >= 0.60. The calibration gave 0.64 here; SE about 0.013.
- G2, beyond text survival: AUC of mass_c's residual on six covariates fitted on the controls >= 0.58. The six are log new words, distinct-4, share cut off, log-prob, first departure and share of unpushed words lost. Calibration: 0.69.
- G3, replication on disjoint questions: rep_AB >= 0.57 and rep_BA >= 0.57. Calibration, with halves of 8 and 4 answers: 0.66 and 0.65.
- G4, the new questions carry a lead of their own: mass on half B alone >= 0.56.
- G5, sanity, all of:
  - the mean AUC over 50 relabelled 512/512 control splits is within 0.47-0.53;
  - the read_gain ratio is within 0.8-1.25;
  - every unit holds all 24 cells;
  - units.jsonl's mass equals the reader's mass_c within 0.002, which shows the code ranks as pre-registered.

L57 probe: the scope extends to L51-57 only if S1-S3 and G5 hold (507/512 units, SE about 0.018).
- S1: mass_c >= 0.58.
- S2: residual >= 0.56.
- S3: rep_AB >= 0.55 and rep_BA >= 0.55.

Decided now for each outcome:
- All pass: whole layers at L40-50, plus 51-57 if the probe passes.
- G1 fails: no whole layer, and the design goes back to the owner. There is no in-between branch.
- G1 passes and G2 fails: the lead is how text survives, not themes; no whole layer.
- G1 and G2 pass and G3 fails: labels belong to the question set. A whole layer would give layer-level leads only and name nothing; the owner decides.
- G4 fails alone: no question swap on this trial's data. The owner chooses between a new trial with new questions and three questions with no held-out half.
- G5 fails: an implementation fault. Fix it and re-read without the GPU (or continue an incomplete run).

max_new rule: the whole layer takes 32 pieces only if the 32-piece reading also passes G1-G5 and lies no more than 0.02 below the 48-piece reading on mass_c and on the lower of rep_AB and rep_BA. Otherwise it takes 48.

Batch rule: as in step 0.

- xq.
